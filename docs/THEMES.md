# Zyphor Color Themes

Zyphor ships with 10 built-in TrueColor (24-bit RGB) palettes designed for optimal contrast, readability, and terminal aesthetics.

## Built-in Palettes
1. **Anthropic (Default)**: Warm amber accents, slate borders, and subtle midnight backgrounds.
2. **Cyberpunk**: Vibrant neon cyan, hot magenta, and deep electric blue borders.
3. **Dracula**: Iconic purple header, vibrant green gauges, and dark violet contrast.
4. **Nord**: Cool arctic blues, frost cyans, and snow-white typography.
5. **Tokyo Night**: Deep indigo storm backdrop with glowing neon accents.
6. **Gruvbox**: Retro warm earthy tones, rich orange highlights, and soft contrast.
7. **Monokai**: Classic hacker green, vivid yellow alerts, and high-contrast dark grey.
8. **Solarized Dark**: Precision optical teal and cyan tones calibrated for eyestrain reduction.
9. **Matrix**: Terminal phosphorescent monochrome green matrix aesthetic.
10. **Plain (No Color)**: Clean ASCII monochrome fallback for standard TTYs and serial consoles.

Press `T` in interactive mode to cycle through themes live.
